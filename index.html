ABOUT S ONE GROUP

S One Group

S One Group is a multi-disciplinary conglomerate dedicated to enhancing the quality of life across multiple sectors. With a foundation built on ethical standards, innovation, and empathy, we deliver excellence in every service we offer.

Our expertise spans travel, healthcare, HR, event management, Interior Design and social impact initiatives, where we combine industry knowledge with innovative solutions to meet evolving client and community needs.

We focus on creating meaningful experiences and sustainable solutions—whether it’s curating memorable travel journeys, providing patient-centric healthcare, managing skilled workforces, executing flawless events, or empowering communities through our charitable initiatives.

ANNUAL EVENT

Clothes & Festive Donation Drive

This festive season, let’s come together to spread warmth, joy, and kindness: Greenation, in association with Tata Steel, is organizing a Clothes & Festive Donation Drive, inviting everyone to contribute and make a difference.

Before Diwali we organize this event annually to bring smiles to the faces of villagers on the outskirts of Jamshedpur and Bhubaneswar.

  • Monetary contributions (for purchasing new clothes)
